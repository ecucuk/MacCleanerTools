Full Disk Access

macOS protects some folders, such as Mail, Safari and app containers, even from apps you run. Without Full Disk Access, MacCleaner cannot read or remove items in them and skips them.

Turn it on

  1. Choose Help › Full Disk Access… and click Open System Settings.
  2. Turn on MacCleaner in the list.
  3. Quit and reopen MacCleaner.
