Getting Started

MacCleaner is a set of tools for disk space and performance. Pick a tool in the sidebar, or press ⌘1 to ⌘8. ⌘0 opens Home.

How every tool works

  1. The tool scans and shows what it found. A scan changes nothing.
  2. You review the list and choose the items.
  3. The tool moves the chosen items to the Trash. You can put them back from the Trash.

Settings

Open MacCleaner › Settings (⌘,) to choose the tool that opens at launch, the appearance, and offline mode.
