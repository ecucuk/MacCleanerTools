Storage Cleaner

Storage Cleaner finds junk in known places: app caches, logs, diagnostic reports, Xcode DerivedData, Archives and DeviceSupport, Simulator caches, and the caches of Homebrew, npm, Yarn and pip.

Use it

  1. Click Scan.
  2. Expand a category and clear the items you want to keep.
  3. Click Clean Selected….

What it never touches

System folders, other users' files, and folders that macOS protects. Items move to the Trash, unless you choose Delete permanently for that clean.
