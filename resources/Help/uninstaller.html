Uninstaller

Uninstaller moves an app and the files it left in your Library to the Trash.

Use it

  1. Select an app.
  2. Review the list. MacCleaner matches by the app's bundle ID and exact name only.
  3. Click Uninstall. A running app is asked to quit first.

Files in /Library are listed as Needs admin and are not removed. Apps that come with macOS are not listed.
